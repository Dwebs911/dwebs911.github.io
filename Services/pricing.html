Our Pricing Plans

At Weber Web Services, we offer two flexible pricing models to suit your business needs. Whether you need a one-time project or ongoing support, we’ve got you covered.

Flat Rate – Pay Once, Own It All

Our flat rate is perfect for businesses that need a website built from the ground up, with everything delivered directly into your hands. You’ll get full ownership of the website and all resources when the project is complete.

  • Full website design and development tailored to your business.
  • Complete documentation and code handover.
  • Responsive, fast, and accessible design (WCAG compliance).
  • No recurring costs – pay once, and the website is yours!

Subscription Service – All-Inclusive Monthly Support

With our subscription model, you don’t have to worry about hosting, updates, or maintenance. We take care of everything, so you can focus on running your business while we handle your online presence.

  • Unlimited Support: Revisions, fixes, and updates whenever needed.
  • Regular Updates: We keep your website fresh, secure, and up to date.
  • Hosting & Maintenance: We host your website on secure, high-performance servers.
