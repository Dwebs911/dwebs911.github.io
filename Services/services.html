Our Services

Build your Website With Weber

At Weber Web Services, we offer a comprehensive approach to web development, design, and IT consulting, guiding you through every step to ensure your business thrives online.

Web Development

We build fast, responsive, and accessible websites. Here's our process:

  • Discovery and Requirements Gathering: We start by understanding your business, target audience, and goals to create a roadmap for development.
  • Planning and Architecture: Creating sitemaps, wireframes, and deciding on the structure to ensure a clear user experience.
  • Development: We write clean, efficient code using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and frameworks, ensuring your site runs smoothly across all devices.
  • SEO and Performance Optimization: We implement SEO best practices, optimize load times, and ensure your site is visible on search engines.
  • Testing & Maintenance: Cross-browser testing, mobile optimization, and ongoing support to keep your site running perfectly.
  • Launch: Finally, we launch your website and provide ongoing support to ensure your success continues.

Web Design

Our design process ensures your website is not only beautiful but highly functional:

  • Creative Discovery: We gather inspiration from your brand, industry, and goals to craft a vision for your website's design.
  • UI/UX Prototyping: Our team creates prototypes to visualize the user journey and interface before implementation.
  • Design Feedback: We present mockups to you, making iterations based on your feedback to perfect the look and feel.
  • Responsive Implementation: We code the design into a responsive website, ensuring it looks great on desktops, tablets, and phones.
  • Design Refinement: Final touches are applied to polish the design, ensuring it represents your brand perfectly.
